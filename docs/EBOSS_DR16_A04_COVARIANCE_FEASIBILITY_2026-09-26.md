### Why 1,000 mocks alone do not establish a 5σ result

For `N` genuinely independent mock realizations and a centered `p`-dimensional data vector, an ordinary sample covariance has rank at most `min(p, N-1)`. Our completed nine-ID code-transport cohort has rank ≤8 for `p=18` and **cannot** support a full 18D sample inverse. Even `N≈1000` does not directly sample a one-sided 5σ null tail: an unsmoothed empirical Monte Carlo tail has resolution no finer than roughly `1/(N+1)` (about `10^{-3}` for 1,000 realizations), while one-sided Gaussian 5σ corresponds to a much rarer probability. An extrapolated significance would require an independently justified and validated distributional/tail model plus robust systematic uncertainty, or a purpose-designed rare-event simulation strategy. Do not label an estimator's `10^{-15}` sign-reversal residual as a statistical sigma.

A large `S/N` cannot be guaranteed by adding mocks or suppressing random sampling noise. Source-matched wake predictions may be intrinsically small or degenerate with the selection model; the correct result could be a calibrated bound or inconclusive measurement.
