# E55 — nine full-eligible mocks with paired 48k random integration

E54 closes the immediate random-floor question for mock0001: four fresh 48k random realizations preserve the F+/F− geometry and pass the preregistered unit-lambda engineering gate in both caps.

E55 therefore expands the **same frozen estimator**, not the physics, to all nine previously fixed EZmock IDs. Each mock/cap uses all source-eligible galaxies and two deterministic fresh 48k random replicas per tracer. R1/R2 are evaluated separately and averaged only after complete marked-LS estimation.

This design measures two noise scales at once: (1) between-mock scatter of the two-replica mean and (2) within-mock random-integration scale from the paired R1−R2 difference.

The primary statistic remains the theory-defined one-dimensional calibrated F−−F+ coordinate. Nine mock means may be summarized by SD/MAD and descriptive symmetric recovery thresholds. They do not support robust tail probabilities, a covariance inverse, detection significance or exclusion.

The preregistered engineering gate requires the paired-random scale to be at most half the between-mock SD in the median across A-D and no larger than the between-mock SD in any field. Because finite source pools cause replica overlap, this is diagnostic rather than a formal variance estimator.

If the gate passes, the next bottleneck is the real external velocity-tag model and amplitude calibration. Observed rows and the observed odd vector remain sealed.
