# E36 — initial hidden kinetic information under collisionless free transport

Date: 2026-09-29. An explicitly toy **linear/external-field, non-self-gravitating** Vlasov free-streaming construction complementing E34/E35. This is not a full Einstein–Vlasov solution, an actual Abacus halo, E27/E28 recalculation or new observed signal. No external input or numerical parameter is selected according to F+ versus F− or observed eBOSS odd data.

Take 1D spatial circle `x∈[0,2π)` and velocity `v∈R`, normalized Gaussian `F0(v)=(2π)^(-1/2)e^(-v²/2)`, `k=q=1` and `a=exp(3/2)/4`. Let `h(v)=cos(v)-a cos(2v)-3 exp(-1/2)/4`. Define two everywhere-positive initial densities `f_±(x,v,0)=F0(v)[1±eps cos(x)h(v)]`, eps=0.1. Bound positivity globally using `eps*(1+a+3exp(-1/2)/4)<1`. Show both share at every x the same zeroth, first and second velocity moments at time zero, but differ as kinetic functions.

Under the exact prescribed-force-free transport `∂_t f+v ∂_x f=0`, `f_±(x,v,t)=f_±(x-vt,v,0)`. The density modes become `rho_±=1±eps cos(x) G(t)` with `G(s)=-exp(-(s²+1)/2)*(cosh(s)-1)^2/2` for k=q=1. Thus G(0)=0 but G(s)<0 for real s≠0. The source is a *homogeneous initial kinetic mode*, distinct from a retarded halo-forcing integral, and its density response begins at order t^4. Initial density, momentum and 1D pressure alone do not fix the later density even with identical external forcing. Caveat: for self-gravitating Vlasov–Poisson, the generated later density changes the potential and this ballistic solution no longer solves the coupled system; for Einstein–Vlasov, equality of Newtonian 0th–2nd moments does not certify matching complete T_mu_nu, gauge or constraints.
